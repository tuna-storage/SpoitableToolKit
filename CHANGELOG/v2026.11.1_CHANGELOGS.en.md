# SpoitableToolKit 2026.11.1

## Heart rate
- The heart rate screen is split into the Now, Statistics, Monitors and Broadcast tabs
- The Monitors tab lists the heart rate monitors found nearby, with their signal strength and when each was last seen
- While receiving from Apple Watch (HDS) is on, the Monitors tab shows the address and port to enter in the Apple Watch app
- The Broadcast tab shows a live preview of each overlay
- The Apple Watch (HDS) settings have a new Firewall item. It shows whether the firewall lets the listening port in, and if it does not, a button adds the rule
- When no monitor is chosen, the app connects to the first monitor it found (before, which one it picked was not fixed)
- Fixed the firewall check and the rule failing when the path of the app's folder contains certain quote characters

## Modules
- The SDK can show a settings item with a status and a button (SpoitableSettingsAction)
