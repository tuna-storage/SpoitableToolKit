# SpoitableToolKit 2026.11.0

The first release.

## Heart rate
- Reads your heart rate from Bluetooth (BLE) monitors and from Health Data Server (HDS) on Apple Watch
- Sends it to your VRChat avatar over OSC (presets: VRCHR, VRCOSC)
- Records it, with statistics and graphs to look back on; exports to CSV
- Broadcasts it over WebSocket for overlays in streaming software

## Video URLs
- When a video fails to play in VRChat, turns its URL into one that plays and copies it (with yt-dlp, or an external server of your own)

## VRChat logs
- Lists what happens in VRChat from its logs: players joining and leaving, worlds, videos and more. Search, filter and go back by date

## VR
- A shortcut bar shows up when you look at your wrist: your heart rate, the time, and shortcuts that open the app's pages
- Opens the app's pages in a VR window, above your wrist or in the space in front of you

## Modules
- Add and update modules from GitHub releases or from source
- Comes with the SDK for writing modules

## App updates
- When a new version is out, a notice shows at the top of the window. Press "Update" to download and install it; the app restarts
- Choose the update channel (Stable / Nightly) in the settings
